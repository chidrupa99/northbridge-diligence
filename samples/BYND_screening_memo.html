Screening Memo — Beyond Meat, Inc. (BYND)

Northbridge deal team · Source: SEC EDGAR · FY2025 basis · July 29, 2026
Notable concerns
Snapshot. Plant-based protein maker (SIC: Food & Kindred Products) in structural decline. FY2025 revenue of $275.5M[S1] is down from $464.7M in FY2021 — a −12.3% five-year CAGR[S1,S3], falling four years running. FY2025 net income is +$219.0M[S1], but the operating business lost −$333.6M in the same year[S1]. Headline profitability is entirely non-operating.
Screen signal: five risk flags fired, three at high severity. The one bright spot is near-term liquidity. Needs an evidenced turnaround thesis to justify further time.

Gross margin has collapsed from ~25% in FY2021[S3] to 2.8%[S1] — the company barely covers its cost of goods. Operating losses narrowed to −$156.1M in FY2024 and then re-widened to −$333.6M in FY2025[S1], so the FY2024 improvement did not hold.

Capital structure

Debt. $415.7M in FY2025[S1] — down $725.7M from FY2024. Approximated as long-term + current debt tags; finance leases and other debt-like items are not fully assembled.
Equity. −$1.0M[S1]: still negative, though up from −$601.2M. There is no equity cushion beneath ~$416M of debt.
Coverage. No operating earnings to cover $14.0M of FY2025 interest expense[S1].
Liquidity. Current ratio 4.6x ($341.5M current assets vs. $74.9M current liabilities)[S1]. Against −$144.9M of annual burn and $203.9M of cash, that is a runway, not a fix.

Risk signals — qualitative (from filing text)

  • The FY2025 10-K's own risk-factor summary leads with "our history of losses and negative cash flows from operating activities" and "weakness in the plant-based meat category, including ongoing and persistent declines in demand"[S1].
  • It also flags "cost-reduction initiatives… workforce reductions and executive leadership changes"[S1] — restructuring in progress and leadership churn.
  • A cluster of 8-Ks filed May–July 2026[S4]. Given the debt movement, read these first.
The one thing to check before anything else. Long-term debt fell $725.7M and negative equity closed by $600.2M in the same year that $552.6M of non-operating profit appeared[S1]. Those three facts are consistent with a debt restructuring or exchange producing a gain on extinguishment — but that is an inference, not something we can source. The item is not isolable from the XBRL data (GainsLossesOnExtinguishmentOfDebt returns no FY2025 value), so the FY2025 income statement and debt footnote need a manual read. If the gain is a one-time restructuring benefit, FY2025 is not a return to profit and the underlying business is still losing roughly a third of a billion dollars a year.
