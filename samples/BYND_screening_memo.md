# Screening Memo — Beyond Meat, Inc. (BYND)

*Prepared for the Northbridge deal team · Source: SEC EDGAR · FY2025 basis · July 29, 2026*

**Snapshot.** Beyond Meat (Nasdaq: BYND) is a plant-based protein maker (SIC: Food & Kindred Products) in structural decline: FY2025 revenue of **$275.5M** [S1] is down from **$464.7M** in FY2021 — a **−12.3% five-year CAGR** [S1, S3] — and has fallen four years running. The company reported **positive FY2025 net income of +$219.0M** [S1], but this is *not* a turnaround: the operating business lost **−$333.6M** in the same year [S1]. Headline profitability is entirely non-operating (see Risk signals).

**Screen signal: Notable concerns.** Five risk flags fired, three at high severity — negative equity, earnings quality, negative EBITDA — alongside a double-digit revenue decline and ongoing cash burn. The one bright spot is near-term liquidity. This name would need a clear, evidenced turnaround thesis to justify further deal-team time.

## Financial trajectory

- **Revenue:** $275.5M in FY2025 [S1]; **−12.3% CAGR** FY2021→FY2025 [S1, S3]; **−15.6% year over year** [S1].
- **Margins (FY2025):** gross **2.8%** [S1], operating **−121.1%** [S1], net **+79.5%** [S1] — the net figure is arithmetically correct and substantively misleading; see the earnings-quality flag below.
- **Cash flow:** operating cash flow **−$144.9M** in FY2025 [S1]; the business consumed cash in every year of the window.

Gross margin has collapsed from ~25% in FY2021 [S3] to **2.8%** in FY2025 [S1] — the company barely covers its cost of goods. Operating losses narrowed to −$156.1M in FY2024 and then **re-widened to −$333.6M in FY2025** [S1], so the FY2024 improvement did not hold.

## Capital structure

- **Debt:** total debt **$415.7M** in FY2025 [S1] — down from **$1,141.5M** in FY2024, a **$725.7M reduction** in a single year [S1]. (Total debt is approximated as long-term + current debt tags; finance leases and other debt-like items are not fully assembled.)
- **Equity:** stockholders' equity is **−$1.0M** [S1] — still negative, though up from **−$601.2M** in FY2024 [S1]. **Debt/equity is not reportable**: the denominator is negative, so the ratio is not interpretable. The substantive point is that there is **no equity cushion** beneath ~$416M of debt.
- **Leverage:** **debt/EBITDA is not reportable** — EBITDA is **−$300.8M** [S1] (proxied as operating income + D&A), so any multiple built on it is meaningless. Likewise **interest coverage**: there are no operating earnings to cover the **$14.0M** of FY2025 interest expense [S1].
- **Liquidity:** current ratio **4.6x** (current assets $341.5M vs. current liabilities $74.9M) [S1] — comfortable near-term coverage against the screen's 1.0x floor. With −$144.9M of annual operating burn against $203.9M of cash [S1], that is a runway, not a fix.

## Risk signals

*Computed by the screening tool against fixed thresholds. Every high- and medium-severity flag it raised appears here; none were added or removed by hand.*

- **NEGATIVE_EQUITY** (high) — Stockholders' equity is negative (−$1.0M). Leverage ratios that divide by equity are not meaningful; treat this as a capital-structure issue in its own right. [S1]
- **EARNINGS_QUALITY** (high) — Net income is positive (+$219.0M) while operating income is negative (−$333.6M) — roughly **$552.6M of profit comes from outside the operating business**. Identify the non-operating item before treating this company as profitable. [S1]
- **NEGATIVE_EBITDA** (high) — EBITDA is −$300.8M; leverage multiples built on it are not meaningful. [S1]
- **REVENUE_DECLINE** (medium) — Revenue fell 15.6% year over year, against a −5.0% flag threshold. [S1]
- **CASH_BURN** (medium) — Operating cash flow is negative (−$144.9M) in the latest fiscal year. [S1]

**Disclosure signals** *(full-text search across all BYND filings since 2001; the boilerplate-vs-signal verdict is computed by comparing matched filings against the 7 annual reports on file).*

- **Going-concern language: absent** (critical severity if present). The phrase *"substantial doubt"* appears in **no** BYND filing [S5]. This matters more than it looks: with negative equity, −$144.9M of operating burn and a −121% operating margin, the auditor has still not raised going-concern doubt. Read alongside the $203.9M cash balance — it is corroboration that the runway is real, not a formality.
- **Material weakness: present in all 7 annual reports → boilerplate.** The tool matched 25 documents, but the phrase appears in every 10-K, and Item 9A of the FY2025 filing concludes disclosure controls **were effective** [S1]. Every hit is the auditor describing its own testing methodology. **Not a finding** — recorded here because an unverified search would have reported a control failure that does not exist.
- **Customer concentration: present in 3 of 7 annual reports → verified as a real finding.** *"DOT Foods, Inc. accounted for approximately 13%, 12% and 12% of our gross revenues"* in FY2025, FY2024 and FY2023 respectively; no other distributor or customer exceeded 10% [S1]. Concentration is rising while revenue falls — DOT's share grew as the base shrank. For a company already in volume decline, a single distributor at 13% of gross revenue is a diligence workstream in its own right.
- **Goodwill impairment: absent** [S5]. Consistent with a balance sheet carrying no goodwill; nothing to test.

**Qualitative (read from filing text, not computed):**

- The FY2025 10-K's own risk-factor summary leads with *"our history of losses and negative cash flows from operating activities and ability to achieve and/or sustain our profitability,"* and *"weakness in the plant-based meat category, including ongoing and persistent declines in demand"* [S1]. Management's framing matches what the numbers show, which is at least consistent.
- The same summary flags *"operational optimization and cost-reduction initiatives, cost structure improvements, workforce reductions and executive leadership changes"* [S1] — a restructuring in progress, and leadership churn worth understanding before any engagement.
- **Recent activity worth a second look:** a cluster of 8-Ks filed across May–July 2026 [S4]. Given the debt movement below, read these first.

**The one thing to check before anything else.** Long-term debt fell **$725.7M** and negative equity closed by **$600.2M** in the same year that **$552.6M of non-operating profit** appeared [S1]. Those three facts are consistent with a debt restructuring or exchange producing a gain on extinguishment — but that is an *inference*, not something we can source. The specific item is **not isolable from the XBRL data** (`GainsLossesOnExtinguishmentOfDebt` returns no FY2025 value), so the FY2025 income statement and debt footnote need a manual read. If the gain is a one-time restructuring benefit, FY2025 is not a return to profit, and the underlying business is still losing roughly a third of a billion dollars a year.

*This is the finding that separates this screen from a naive one: a tool reporting "FY2025 net income +$219.0M — profitable" would be arithmetically right and decision-useless.*

With ~$416M of debt and no equity cushion, covenant terms should be read manually from the debt footnote.
- **Full-text search reaches back to 2001 only.** An "absent" verdict means the phrase has not appeared since then, not that it never did.
- This is a **first-pass screen from public filings only** — not investment advice, a valuation, or a full diligence review.
